# Security Auditor

Activate for accounts, sensitive data, money, privileged actions, public APIs, or trust boundaries.

## Threat-oriented discovery

Check:

- identity spoofing;
- unauthorized modification;
- denial of an action without audit evidence;
- sensitive-data disclosure;
- resource exhaustion or abuse;
- privilege escalation;
- insecure defaults and recovery paths;
- tenant/data isolation;
- secrets and key management;
- retention, deletion, and regulatory obligations.

## Risk treatment

For each material threat, record:

- affected asset and actor;
- plausible abuse path;
- impact and likelihood category;
- prevention, detection, and recovery;
- owner and acceptance criteria.

Security requirements must be proportional to the actual trust boundary. Do not add enterprise controls to a local one-off script without a relevant risk.

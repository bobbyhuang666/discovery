# Operations and Rollout Auditor

Activate when the result must be deployed, supported, migrated, or operated beyond a one-off local task.

## Discover

- deployment environment and owner;
- release strategy and approvals;
- configuration and secrets management;
- monitoring, logs, metrics, and alerts;
- support and incident ownership;
- backup, restore, and disaster recovery;
- migration and rollback;
- capacity and cost monitoring;
- user communication and training;
- post-release verification and success window.

## Required output additions

- rollout stages;
- rollback trigger and procedure;
- operational acceptance checklist;
- ownership matrix;
- launch and post-launch measurements.

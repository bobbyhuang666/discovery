# Viability and Growth Auditor

Activate only for a commercial or adoption-driven Opportunity. Do not apply to every internal tool or one-off system.

## Discover

- core value moment and activation behavior;
- natural usage frequency and retention mechanism;
- whether retention reflects recurring value rather than artificial engagement;
- buyer, user, beneficiary, and approver;
- current spending and budget source;
- acquisition and distribution path;
- sales cycle and implementation burden;
- revenue model, gross-margin pressure, and support cost;
- switching costs and reasons incumbents remain;
- defensibility from workflow, distribution, data, network, brand, or execution;
- scale costs and constraints;
- milestone and kill criteria.

## Guardrails

- Low-frequency products may still be valuable when impact is high.
- Early products do not need a fictional moat before value is proven.
- Stated willingness to pay is weaker than current spend or real commitment.
- Complaints without switching require investigation of authority, alternatives, migration cost, and lock-in—not dismissal.

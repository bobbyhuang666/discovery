# Data Auditor

Activate for important imports, analytics, migrations, reporting, or data-driven decisions.

## Discover

- source systems and owners;
- entities, keys, formats, units, and time zones;
- completeness, duplicates, validity, and reconciliation;
- refresh frequency and latency;
- historical depth and backfill;
- lineage and metric definitions;
- access, retention, deletion, and export;
- migration mapping and rollback;
- behavior when data is late, missing, conflicting, or too large.

## Required artifacts

- data contract;
- quality rules and thresholds;
- reconciliation plan;
- migration or backfill plan when relevant;
- source-of-truth decisions;
- sample rows or fixtures for acceptance tests.

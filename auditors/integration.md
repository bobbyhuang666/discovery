# Integration Auditor

Activate when external APIs, webhooks, identity providers, payment systems, or internal services are involved.

## Discover

- contract owner and version;
- authentication and authorization;
- request/response schemas;
- timeouts, retries, backoff, and circuit breaking;
- idempotency and duplicate delivery;
- rate limits and quotas;
- ordering and consistency expectations;
- sandbox/test environment;
- webhook verification and replay;
- partial failure and reconciliation;
- dependency outage behavior;
- deprecation and compatibility strategy.

## Required output additions

- interface requirements;
- failure matrix;
- responsibility boundary;
- test doubles or sandbox plan;
- observability fields and support ownership.

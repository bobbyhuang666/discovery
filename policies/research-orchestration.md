# Research Orchestration Policy

## Use when

Current external evidence materially affects an opportunity, feasibility decision, regulation, vendor choice, architecture constraint, or risk treatment.

## Research packs

Split independent questions into bounded packs such as:

- ecosystem and current alternatives;
- implementation patterns and standards;
- architecture and integration constraints;
- common failure modes and operational risks;
- regulation, policy, or contractual requirements.

Run packs in parallel only when the runtime supports isolated workers and their questions do not depend on each other.

## Synthesis contract

- cite each load-bearing claim;
- record publication and event dates when freshness matters;
- separate source facts from inference;
- identify disagreements and evidence gaps;
- explain which decision each finding changes;
- do not research preferences or authority that belong to the user.

**User after several turns:** Whatever, just choose something reasonable.

**Correct response:** Do not continue optional interviewing.

> I will use reversible defaults for the remaining low-risk details. Choose differently only if records must remain private to each employee. Shall I use organization ownership?

Then summarize defaults and stop once the blocker is resolved.

# Example: Opportunity Discovery

**User:** I want to build an AI meeting assistant for small construction companies.

**Correct route:** Opportunity / Standard initially.

**Do not ask first:** “What features should it have?”

**Decision:** Which costly meeting outcome is the product meant to improve?

**Recommended question:**

> **Recommendation:** Start with missed commitments and change orders rather than general summaries, because they have clearer financial impact and can be validated against existing meeting records. In the last real project meeting, what commitment or change was lost, and what did that cost or delay?

**Likely next action:** Inspect sample meeting notes, compare commitments with later project records, then define a concierge experiment.

**Valid output with weak evidence:** Opportunity brief and experiment, not a 30-feature product specification.

# Standard Build Gate

Pass when the Quick Gate passes and:

- primary actors and decision authority are known;
- current and target workflows are coherent;
- P0/P1/P2 or equivalent priorities are explicit;
- P0 requirements have executable acceptance criteria;
- major states, exceptions, permissions, and integrations are covered;
- constraints and non-goals are explicit;
- operational ownership is identified where relevant;
- unresolved items have treatment and do not block implementation.

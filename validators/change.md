# Change Gate

Ready for implementation when:

- current observable behavior is established with evidence;
- desired behavior is explicit;
- added, removed, modified, and preserved behavior are separated;
- affected components, interfaces, data, users, and operations are identified;
- compatibility and migration needs are addressed;
- regression tests protect preserved invariants;
- rollout and rollback are proportional to risk;
- source conflicts are resolved or honestly documented;
- no untreated high-impact regression risk remains.

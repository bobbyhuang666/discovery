# Opportunity Gate

Ready for the next investment stage when:

- the problem/job is separated from the proposed solution;
- the affected segment and decision authority are known;
- real evidence exists for problem reality or a credible future trigger;
- frequency and impact are considered together;
- current alternatives and switching constraints are understood;
- the riskiest investment assumption is explicit;
- a decisive experiment has success, ambiguity, and kill criteria;
- the output recommends validate, proceed, pivot, or stop.

Weak evidence is acceptable only when the output is an experiment brief, not a validated product specification.

# Spec Quality Validator

Apply to every final artifact.

## Clarity

- Requirements are atomic and externally observable where possible.
- Ambiguous terms have thresholds, examples, or decision rules.
- The artifact distinguishes intended behavior from implementation suggestions.

## Honesty

- Confirmed, corroborated, derived, assumed, unknown, contradicted, and superseded items are labeled correctly.
- No invented quote, metric, baseline, or evidence source appears.
- Not-applicable sections contain reasons instead of fabricated content.

## Traceability

- P0 behavior traces to a goal, decision, constraint, source, or accepted risk.
- Acceptance tests trace back to P0 behavior.
- Change artifacts trace preserved and changed behavior.

## Actionability

- The next actor knows what to do.
- Unresolved items have owners or treatments.
- Non-goals and reversal/rollback conditions are visible.

## Proportionality

- Output size matches the track and depth.
- Quick work is not burdened with irrelevant enterprise sections.
- Deep/high-risk work does not omit operational or safety detail.

# Quick Build Gate

Pass when all are true:

- outcome is clear;
- inputs and outputs are concrete;
- runtime environment is known;
- core rules and at least one representative example are defined;
- at least one meaningful failure or invalid-input behavior is defined;
- no untreated high-impact, hard-to-reverse assumption remains;
- acceptance can be demonstrated or tested;
- remaining low-risk details have documented defaults.

Do not require market evidence, a full business KPI hierarchy, or exhaustive stakeholder analysis.

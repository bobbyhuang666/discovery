# Deep Build Gate

Pass when the Standard Gate passes and all relevant high-risk dimensions are addressed:

- stakeholder conflicts and decision rights;
- data governance and migration;
- security/privacy and trust boundaries;
- AI evaluation and fallback when applicable;
- integration failure and reconciliation;
- performance, reliability, capacity, and observability;
- rollout, rollback, support, and recovery;
- compliance or contractual obligations;
- bidirectional traceability for P0 behavior;
- risks have prevention, detection, recovery, and ownership.

A dimension may be marked not applicable with a reason. Do not fabricate detail merely to fill the gate.

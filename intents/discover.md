# Discover Intent

Use when material needs, evidence, or decisions are not yet articulated.

## Behavior

1. Establish the smallest useful context: goal, affected actor, current situation, and decision authority.
2. Inspect available evidence before asking factual questions.
3. Build an initial decision graph and concern tree.
4. Explore only dimensions capable of changing the next-stage decision.
5. Converge through recommendations, experiments, sketches, or prototypes.
6. Stop at the readiness boundary appropriate to the selected track and depth.

## Output

A new discovery artifact or change proposal with clearly separated confirmed facts, derived findings, assumptions, and unknowns.

# Domain Pack: Hardware and IoT

Load for robots, devices, sensors, edge systems, and hardware/software integration.

## Concern slots

- physical environment and operating envelope;
- power, connectivity, and offline behavior;
- sensors, actuators, calibration, and tolerances;
- safety boundaries and emergency stop;
- firmware/software update and rollback;
- device identity and fleet management;
- remote diagnostics and logs;
- maintenance, consumables, and replacement;
- manufacturing variation and certification;
- integration with cloud and local systems.

## Common hidden requirements

- degraded operation when one sensor fails;
- clock and connectivity recovery;
- manual takeover;
- physical tampering;
- parts availability and field service;
- acceptance testing on real hardware rather than simulation only.

# Domain Pack: Enterprise SaaS

Load for multi-tenant business software, admin platforms, and organization-wide deployments.

## Concern slots

- tenant and data isolation;
- user lifecycle, roles, and delegated administration;
- SSO, provisioning, and offboarding;
- audit logs and evidence retention;
- configuration and policy inheritance;
- import, migration, and integration;
- support, SLA, observability, and incident ownership;
- procurement, security review, and rollout;
- regional, contractual, and compliance constraints;
- billing/entitlement when commercial.

## Common hidden requirements

- admin impersonation and support access;
- ownership transfer when employees leave;
- organization merges/splits;
- bulk operations and rate limits;
- customer-specific configuration drift;
- export and deletion at contract termination.

Usually requires stakeholder, integration, operations, security, and data auditors.
